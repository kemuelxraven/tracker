One honest addition to the palette. All five swatches are mid-tones — the darkest, Beaver #AC7E6E, reaches only 2.5:1 against Champagne pink, where readable body text needs 4.5:1. Swap it for your own dark if you prefer — it is used in exactly one place, the type.

Description

The long body of the listing

Your money, on one page. Nobody else's servers.

Most budget apps ask you to sign up, hand over your bank login, and trust a company you have never met with the most private numbers you own. This one does not. It opens in your browser, saves everything on your own device, and works whether or not you have signal.

Built for how people actually keep money.
Two wallets, side by side — one for the money in your bank or e-wallet, one for the cash physically in your pocket. Rename them to match your life: GCash, Savings, Payroll, Baon. Move money between them and it is never counted twice, never mistaken for spending.

A budget you build, not one you guess at.
Your monthly budget is not a number you type in — it is the sum of every deposit you record. Add your pay, add the side income, and the total is simply the truth. Split it into categories with their own monthly amounts, tick "use this amount every month", and the whole thing rebuilds itself on payday. Set your budget month to start on the 15th, or the 30th, or whenever you actually get paid.

Loans that remind you before you forget.
Add the loan, the lender, the total, and the day of the month it is due. The tracker watches that date for you: quiet while it is far off, a reminder on the day, a warning when it is late, and green the moment you log a payment. When the payments add up to the total, it marks itself Paid.

Savings goals that stop you spending them.
Name what you are saving for, put money aside, and that amount stops counting as money you can still spend — so nothing gets promised twice.

Reports you can actually read.
Search every record you have ever logged. See money out, money in and the net for any month or any range. Four charts of the same records: spending by category, money in against money out, the trend across months, and where it all went. Export transactions, category budgets or a monthly summary as CSV, or print the monthly report as a PDF to send to whoever needs it.

Private, and locked if you want it.
Set a four-digit PIN and the tracker locks itself after a few minutes of sitting idle. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is analysed, nothing is sold. There is no account to create because there is no server to create it on.

Yours to keep, and to make your own.
Change the title, the subtitle, the logo and the whole colour scheme from one colour. Light and dark are worked out for you. Add your own background image and dial its strength down until it is exactly as quiet as you want.

Install it like an app.
Add it to your home screen on a phone or tablet and it opens full-screen with no address bar, offline, in about a second. It also runs happily in any modern browser on a laptop.

How it works
It is a web app you open in your browser. There is no installer and no account. Open it once and your device keeps a copy, so it works with no internet from then on. On a phone or tablet you can add it to your home screen and it behaves like any other app.

Where your data lives
On your device, in your browser's own storage. It never leaves. That is the point — and it is also the one thing you have to take seriously: you are the only backup. The tracker exports a single backup file in one tap. Take one on day one and one a month after that, and you are safe. Clearing your browser data, using a private window, or switching to a new phone without a backup will lose your entries.
