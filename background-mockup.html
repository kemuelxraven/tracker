Theme backgrounds

Six themes, each with a light and a dark board at 1920 × 1080 px. Rebuild them in Canva from the spec under each board — every shape has its real pixel position, its hex and its opacity, so nothing needs eyedroppering. The colours come from the same formula the tracker uses to build a theme, so a background made to this spec will sit under its theme rather than fight it.

Using these

  • Export each board from Canva as PNG at 1920 × 1080.
  • In the tracker: Settings → Look of this tracker → Background → Choose a picture.
  • The picture is stored on the device separately from your records, shrunk to 1600 px and saved as a JPEG, so a large photo can never stop your budget saving.
  • A custom background starts at 35% strength on purpose — it is there to give the page a mood, not to compete with the figures. The slider takes it up or down.
  • Light and dark are separate boards: set whichever matches the mode you use most, or keep the built-in artwork and just change the colour theme.
